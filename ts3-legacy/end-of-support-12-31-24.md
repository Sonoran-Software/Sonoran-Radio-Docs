# End Of Support 12/31/24

## Sonoran Radio: TeamSpeak 3 End-Of-Support

**As of 12/31/2023 Sonoran Radio's TeamSpeak 3 (Legacy) version will no longer receive any support for users, customers, community owners, etc.**

Additionally, new community registrations will be restricted to the standalone version only. **No new TS3 based community registrations will be available beyond this date.**

## End Of Life Date

While the TeamSpeak 3 plugin, in-game resource, and web portal will remain active for some time - communities are strongly encouraged to migrate to the new Standalone version.

**In the event of a breaking update from FiveM/CFX or Teamspeak 3, Sonoran Radio will become unusable** as no new updates or customer support will be provided.

## Why Switch to Standalone?

Sonoran Radio Standalone offers a vastly improved radio communications simulator, without the added costs or requirements of TeamSpeak 3.

With a pre-configured resource download available directly in the radio panel, it only takes minutes to be up and running!
